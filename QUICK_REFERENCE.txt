   File:          src/attention_model.py (SpatialAttentionModule class)
   Why it matters: Makes model INTERPRETABLE

2. CHANNEL ATTENTION MODULE (CAM)
   What it does:  Shows WHICH features matter for detection
   File:          src/attention_model.py (ChannelAttentionModule class)
   Why it matters: Explains model reasoning

3. FOCAL LOSS
   What it does:  Handles extreme class imbalance (83:16 ratio)
   File:          src/focal_loss.py
   Why it matters: Focuses training on minority class (anomalies)
   Formula:       L = -(1-p_t)^2 * log(p_t)

4. OPTIMAL THRESHOLD TUNING
   What it does:  Finds best decision boundary
   File:          src/train_attention_focal.py
   Why it matters: Adapted to YOUR data distribution
   Result:        0.5017 (not fixed 0.5)
